LVGL Kft. LVGL is not included here. Each sample pulls it in as a dependency.

## Fonts in the app samples and screenshots

`examples/showcase/fonts` holds bitmap fonts made with lv_font_conv from two sources. These files
keep their sources' licences, not this repository's MIT licence.

- **Inter** by The Inter Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1, in
  [`LICENSE-Inter.txt`](examples/showcase/fonts/LICENSE-Inter.txt).
- **Lucide** icons by Lucide Icons and Contributors, ISC licence. Some of the icons used (such as
  check, chevron, clock, info, lock, music, power, search and tv) come from Feather by Cole Bemis
  under the MIT licence. Both licences are in
  [`LICENSE-Lucide.txt`](examples/showcase/fonts/LICENSE-Lucide.txt).

The app sample screenshots are set in those fonts. The demo screenshots show the Montserrat fonts
built into LVGL (SIL Open Font License 1.1). LVGL's widget symbols come from Font Awesome Free,
whose fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and its icons under CC BY 4.0. Montserrat and
Font Awesome are not included here; they come with LVGL.

The GlassVGL wordmark in `assets/logo/` is set in Inter Display SemiBold, copyright The Inter Project
Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1. The logo files hold the letters as outlines, and no Inter font
file is included.
